current files. Decide exactly what to change. For each step give the file, the function, its
full new signature on one line, and the change.

  like `*`. If a fact says an argument is required, it gets no default.

  a dict), not by adding a new parameter.
- Only plan what the ticket asks for. Keep every other function as it is.
- If the ticket asks to ship a release, add steps for that using the facts.
- Do not write code.

Reply with JSON: {"steps": [{"file": "...", "function": "...", "new_signature": "...", "change": "..."}]}
